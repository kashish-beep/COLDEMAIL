
# Cold Email Campaign Sandbox

A B2B cold email outreach project focused on lead generation, audience segmentation, personalized email campaigns, A/B testing, and campaign performance tracking.

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a structured cold email campaign targeting HR Heads, HR Managers, and Recruiters across different industries.

The objective is to organize potential business contacts, create targeted email sequences, and track campaign engagement through a simple reporting system.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Apollo.io:** B2B lead generation and contact research.
- **Google Sheets:** Lead management, segmentation, data cleaning, and campaign tracking.
- **Gmail:** Drafting and managing cold email outreach.

## Key Features

### 1. Lead Generation & Segmentation
- Created a B2B lead list targeting HR professionals and recruiters.
- Segmented contacts based on job roles and industries.
- Organized contact information for targeted outreach.

### 2. Data Hygiene
- Identified and removed duplicate contacts.
- Checked email validity.
- Maintained suppression lists for bounced emails and unsubscribed contacts.

### 3. Cold Email Sequence
- Developed a three-step cold email sequence.
- Created alternative subject lines and calls-to-action.
- Structured follow-ups to maintain engagement without excessive messaging.

### 4. A/B Testing
- Designed alternative subject lines to compare email engagement.
- Tested different calls-to-action to evaluate recipient responses.

### 5. Campaign Performance Tracking
Tracked key campaign metrics:
- Open Rate
- Reply Rate
- Click Rate
- Bounce Rate
- Unsubscribe Rate

Organized campaign metrics into a weekly reporting tracker.

## Campaign Workflow

1. Identify potential B2B contacts using Apollo.io.
2. Segment leads based on persona and industry.
3. Clean and organize contact data using Google Sheets.
4. Develop personalized cold email sequences.
5. Manage outreach through Gmail.
6. Track engagement metrics and prepare weekly reports.
